Bounty rules

  • Verify, never claim. A matching fingerprint is your proof of concept — stop there.
  • Policy decides. Most programs want fingerprint evidence in the report, not a completed takeover.
  • 404 ≠ vulnerable. Only a service's known unclaimed fingerprint counts.
  • Dangling ≠ proven. A CNAME whose target doesn't resolve needs manual investigation.
  • Probe only with permission. Active SQLi/XSS checks run attack payloads — same scope rules, no exceptions.
  • Stay in scope. Out-of-scope domains are off limits, no exceptions.
TakeoverLens performs passive reconnaissance only (certificate transparency data, public DNS, HTTP fingerprinting). It cannot and will not claim third-party resources. Fingerprint dataset: curated subset of can-i-take-over-xyz.
